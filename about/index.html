Our Story

About Perry Brothers Equipment

Since 1991, we've been buying and selling construction equipment throughout the world. Over the years, we have sold thousands of pieces of equipment and built lasting relationships with satisfied customers.

Perry Brothers Equipment offers a full line of high-quality construction machinery, including SANY equipment, used machines, rentals, parts, and service support. Our goal is to make the equipment buying and rental process simple, transparent, and dependable.

As a family-owned business, we work to earn trust through honest communication, careful inspections, and responsive support before and after the sale.
