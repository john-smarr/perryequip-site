Understanding the True Value of a Mini Excavator: Is It Worth It?

Home›Media›Understanding the True Value of a Mini Excavator

The prices in this article are rough estimates. Please contact us for accurate and up-to-date information!

Investing in heavy equipment like a mini excavator is a major business decision. While return on investment (ROI) is a key metric, it is not the only one that matters. Beyond the initial purchase price, long-term factors like durability, reliability, maintenance, and operating costs all play a role in determining the lifetime value of your equipment.

So, what exactly is the long-term value of a SANY SY35U mini excavator? We can break it down—whether you are a seasoned pro or buying your first piece of equipment—and help you make an informed decision.

Why the SANY SY35U?

It is simple: the SY35U is one of SANY's top-performing and most popular mini excavators. With an operating weight of 8,499 lbs. and powered by a 24 HP Yanmar engine, and built for versatility—ideal for landscaping, agriculture, plumbing, and general contracting. It is compact enough for residential and utility work but powerful enough for tough excavation tasks.

Cost of Ownership

Initial Investment

Of course, there are the initial upfront costs of buying your machine and necessary attachments. Here is a breakdown of the primary costs you can expect when purchasing a SANY SY35U:

  • SY35U Excavator: ~$60,000
  • Bucket: ~$1,200
  • Optional Quick Coupler: ~$3,500
  • Optional Hydraulic Thumb: ~$3,500
  • Total: ~$68,200

One of SANY's biggest advantages is that many features other brands charge extra for—like LED lighting, auxiliary hydraulics, and HVAC systems—are standard with SANY. Since these are included, you do not have to worry about paying extra for them.

Interest

If you are financing your machine, you will need to factor in interest costs. Interest rates vary, so you should research current trends and rates.

Other Business Costs

There will be other costs you will need to consider. These will vary, so these are some common examples:

  • Sales Tax—Most states will have sales tax, and the amount varies.
  • Tax Deduction—There are tax deductions available for equipment costs to keep in mind.
  • Insurance—Typically, around 2% of machine value, often included in your business insurance.

Warranty Benefits

SANY offers an industry-leading 5-year / 5,000-hour warranty on new machines. That means you get peace of mind with comprehensive coverage and fewer worries about surprise repair bills. This comes standard with your machine, so you will not need to buy an extended warranty like with other brands.

Operating Costs

Even if you only use your excavator 700 hours per year, operational costs are a major factor in calculating ROI. There are many things to keep in mind when pricing out a client:

  • The excavator
  • Fuel
  • Lubrication
  • Maintenance
  • Labor
  • Fees (transportation, dump, permit)
  • The profit margin you want to charge for the job.

Fuel

You will need to have enough fuel to run your machine and any attachments you use each day. This cost will fluctuate as diesel prices change. You will use about 1 tank of fuel per 8 hours. We will use the average price of diesel ($3.51) as of April 2025. Here is how that averages out per year based on these prices:

  • Fuel tank capacity: 6 gallons
  • Average diesel cost: $3.51/gallon
  • Daily fuel cost (8 hours): ~$37.22
  • Daily fuel cost (4 hours): ~$18.61
  • Annual fuel cost (700 hours): ~$3,260

Lubrication

Proper lubrication ensures that moving pieces move when and where they should, lightening the strain on the solid parts. Sufficient lubrication of your machine parts is necessary to ensure your machine continues to run smoothly. A good rule to follow for your SANY SY35U is to use 2 pumps per fitting every 10 hours—or 1 tube of grease per normal day of work. For a 700-hour annual goal (approximately 200 days at 4 hours/day), your budget should be:

  • 1 tube of grease/day: ~$10
  • Annual lubrication: ~$2,000

Maintenance & Service

Oil Changes

Oil changes are just a part of owning any heavy equipment. You should follow the owner's manual suggestions for intervals and hour requirements. Sending oil samples to your dealer can often be part of your manufacturer-recommended warranty as well. Maintaining the manufacturer-recommended service intervals will help you keep your machine up and running at peak performance and avoid your warranty from being void.

Routine Service

Appropriate maintenance intervals are key to keeping your warranty valid, as well as keeping your machine running smoothly. You will find service interval instructions in your owner's manual, as each machine will have different intervals. SANY offers service kits for your SY35U to make it easy:

  • 50 hours—Your first service will be at the 50-hour mark.
  • Every 250 hours—Change your engine oil and filter.
  • 500 hours—Everything on the engine, including the fuel, oil, and air filters. SANY offers a 500-hour kit for purchase for ~$170.
  • 1,000 hours—Everything including hydraulic filters (return filter, suction filter, and pilot filter). SANY's 1,000-hour kit runs ~$310.
  • Up to 2,000 hours—You will need to spend ~$1,350 on filters, and extra for your oil changes.
  • Cabin filters—Change filters as necessary.

Replacement Parts & Wear Items

Wear and tear on parts is a common and inevitable part of owning a machine. You eventually need to replace some parts, even if you take perfect care of them. Proper maintenance, careful operation, and climate-protected storage (to avoid heat cracking in tracks) will all help extend the life of your parts. Parts that might need to be replaced include:

  • Rubber Tracks—Replace rubber tracks every 2,500 hours for ~$2,500.
  • Consumables (teeth, edges, cutting blades)—Cost and frequency depend on use and terrain.

Resale Value

SANY machines hold their value well because of their durability and market reputation. When it comes time to sell, factors like age, condition, hours used, attachments, and market demand all influence your return. The SY35U's compatibility with multiple attachments boosts its appeal in the resale market.

In a healthy market, a well-maintained machine used for 3,500 hours can resell for $20,000–$25,000.

Total ROI & Hourly Cost Breakdown

Now to the big question: What does this all mean for ROI? We will assume you run the SY35U 700 hours/year for 5 years, totaling 3,500 hours. Here is a simplified cost breakdown:

Category 5-Year Total Hourly Cost (3,500 hours)
Purchase & Attachments ~$68,200 ~$19.49
Fuel ~$3,260/year x 5 = ~$16,300 ~$4.66
Maintenance/Service ~$2,500 ~$0.71
Insurance (2%/year) ~$5,200 ~$1.49
Replacement Tracks (1 set) ~$2,450 ~$0.70
Estimated Total Cost ~$94,450 ~$26.99/hr.

These are rough estimates and may vary based on machine price changes, fuel prices, labor costs, and intensity of use.

We can assume the resale value of your machine in a healthy market will be around $25,000. This is assuming you used your machine for the 3,500 hours and left it in good condition. This value will help lower your total costs.

Category 5-Year Total
Previous Estimated Total Cost ~$94,450
Resale Value ~$25,000
Estimated Total Value ~$69,450

$69,450 divided by 3,500 hours leaves us with a total hourly cost of $19.84 per hour. How about that for value?

Is the SANY SY35U Worth It?

With standard features included, an industry-leading warranty, and strong resale potential, the SY35U offers excellent long-term value. Compared to competitors with limited warranties and extra costs for essentials, the SY35U helps you control costs from day one—while giving you reliability for years.

Whether you are purchasing your first excavator or adding to your fleet, understanding the total cost of ownership and hourly ROI is essential. The SY35U delivers robust performance with manageable costs, making it a smart investment for businesses focused on productivity and profitability.
