SANY Working Modes Explained: How to Maximize Fuel Efficiency

Home›Media›SANY Working Modes Explained

In forestry work, the right attachments for your equipment can make a world of difference. The right tools make the job faster, safer, and boost efficiency. They also reduce the frustration that comes with using the wrong gear. Perry Brothers Equipment is here to help you find the right forestry attachments for your job and machine.

Type of Forestry Attachments

1. Mulchers are attachments for skid steers and excavators. They are invaluable tools and will help you with land clearing and undergrowth management. Mulchers grind up small trees, shrubs, and underbrush into mulch, leaving the ground ready for bigger and heavier tasks and projects.

2. Brush Cutters are another attachment for skid steers and excavators that cut through thicker vegetation than mulchers. They are perfect for cutting through dense brushes, saplings, and smaller trees. Brush cutters slice through tougher material with ease and leave behind a clear path, allowing you to move on to managing larger trees.

3. Tree Pullers and Tree Shears are more attachments for skid steers and excavators, and they specialize in uprooting or cutting down medium-sized trees. Tree pullers grab and pull smaller trees out by the roots, while tree shears slice through the trunks of medium-sized trees. Tree shears offer fast and cleaner cutting, making them perfect for clearing areas so you can reach large trees.

4. Felling Heads and Harvesting Heads are high-efficiency attachments for excavators. They cut and process large trees. These attachments grab the tree, cut it, and either drop it or process it in one motion. Harvesting heads strip branches and can even cut logs to length right on the spot, improving the speed and efficiency of your forestry operations.

5. Log Forks are attachments for wheel loaders and skid steers, and great for moving and stacking logs quickly and efficiently. With these attachments, you can grab multiple logs at once. This will speed up the process and keep your operation flowing smoothly.

6. Grapples are attachments for wheel loaders and excavators. They move large logs, stumps, branches, and brushes. They clamp down securely on materials, so you can haul heavy loads safer, faster, and more efficiently.

Matching Your Attachment to Your Machine

Each machine has specific hydraulic and power requirements. No matter what machine you have, use the correct attachment. This will help ensure you get the best performance from your machine. If you use a larger attachment on a smaller machine, make sure your machine has adequate hydraulic flow and counterbalance. This is important to manage the load without strain. You should also check the attachment's flow ratings and capacity requirement to avoid straining your machine.

Some SANY models include standard two-way auxiliary hydraulics, making things easier for you. On machines like the SY35 and above, you can add a three-way valve, achieving continuous flow, allowing you to use a wide range of attachments. However, more specialized tools like felling heads may still require a larger machine to achieve their highest performance.

Streamlining Your Operation

Choosing the right forestry attachments for your machine is important for getting your job done. More than that, it is about getting it done more efficiently, safely, and with greater ease. Each attachment serves a unique purpose and, when used correctly, can help tackle any forestry task.
